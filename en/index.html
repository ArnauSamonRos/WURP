            About

            Why WURP exists

            We started as six people running in Ciutadella park on Thursday evenings. Now there are hundreds of us. Each with their own goal: a first 10K, a sub-1:30 half, coming back from an injury.

            We realised what moved us forward wasn't the kit or the watch. It was having a clear goal and people to work on it with. That's WURP: social clubs where nobody gets left behind, kit made to last and challenges you earn through consistency.

            It's not about being faster than anyone. It's about knowing what your goal is. What's ur peak?
